RESULTS SO FAR (seed 1, Dolly test set)
---------------------------------------

1) Caching fewer teacher probabilities makes students less varied and worse
   calibrated, with no change in quality (H1).

   Forward KL               Top-8    Top-32   Top-128   Top-8 + tail bucket
   Quality (ROUGE-L)         18.5     18.4     18.4      18.4
   Variety (semantic div.)   0.316    0.337    0.349     0.348
   Repetitiveness (S-BLEU)   13.8     11.1     10.0      10.2
   Tail coverage             0.52     0.61     0.64      0.61
   Calibration error (ECE)   0.106    0.075    0.065     0.068

   Every diversity and calibration measure moves in order as k grows.

2) The existing tail-bucket ("ghost token") fix recovers almost all of it (H4).
   One extra number per token makes top-8 behave like top-128. It restores the
   AMOUNT of tail mass, but less of WHICH tail tokens get it.

3) Reverse KL from the base model fails to stop generating: about 1% of
   answers end. Top-8 RKL produces noisy text. Expected; reported as decided.

4) Seq-KD is the least varied and worst calibrated student (ECE 0.257).
   SFT has the highest ROUGE-L (27.6) because it copies Dolly's short answers.

5) PopQA shows no difference between cache sizes (48-49%). Exploratory; likely
   because Dolly contains too few rare facts.
