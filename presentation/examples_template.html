What the students actually write

The same test prompts answered by the 1.7B teacher and by 135M students trained with different teacher signals. Nothing here is hand-picked: prompts are a fixed random draw from the 500-item test set.

Each card is one position in a real test answer, scored with the gold text as input. The gold next word sits outside the teacher's top 8, so a top-8 cache never shows it to the student. The rows show what probability each model gives to the teacher's top 16 words there.
